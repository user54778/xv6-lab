1) Why are there missing keys with 2 threads but not 1?
A) There is a data race. There are critical sections in put() and insert() that need 
to be done atomically, but as is, nothing is preventing multiple threads from operating
on the same data, resulting in lost data.
Sequence (put): t1 checks if not placed, notes not, but then at the same time, t2 interrupts it, places its
value, and t1 multiplexes back to its thread and then places its value. 
2) We use a lock per hash bucket to take advantage of scenario where concurrent put()s have no overlap
in memory.
